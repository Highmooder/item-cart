1. Who we are

("we", "us") runs an online marketplace that connects students with vendors around their campus. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. You can contact us at .

2. What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, email address, and, for delivery, your delivery location and any note you add. We also record what you ordered and your order and payment status.
  • When you create an account: your name, email, phone number, and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider; we can't read it.
  • When you message a vendor: the messages you send. Phone numbers typed into a message are hidden from the other person.
  • When you apply to sell: your store name, your name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, email, location and a description of your business.
  • When you pay online: Paystack, our payment provider, collects your card or bank details. We never see or store your card number. We keep the payment reference, status, amount and any fee.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends (such as your IP address) is handled by our hosting and database providers to run the service and keep it secure.

3. How we use it

  • To take and fulfil your orders, confirm payments, arrange delivery or pickup, and handle refunds and disputes.
  • To let vendors and customers message each other about an order.
  • To review vendor applications and pay vendors for completed orders.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent fraud and spam, and meet legal and accounting duties.
  • To send service messages such as sign-in and order emails.

4. Who we share it with

  • Vendors you buy from see your name, what you ordered, and (for delivery) your delivery location and order note. They do not see your phone number or email through the site; they contact you through in-site messages.
  • Paystack processes online payments for us.
  • Service providers that run the site for us: our database and sign-in provider (Supabase) and our hosting provider (Netlify). They process data only to provide those services.
  • Google, only if you use Ask Item cart AI (see below).
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information. Our providers may process data in countries outside Nigeria.

5. Ask Item cart AI

Ask Item cart AI helps you find products. When you ask it a question, the words you type are sent to Google's Gemini service so it can work out what you are looking for (for example an item, a budget and a place). To help with follow-up questions it also sends the names of products already shown in your chat (not their prices), and if you ask a question about one product, that product's public listing text. It never sends your name, phone, email, orders or account. The products and prices you see come from our own catalog, not from the AI.

Because we use Google's free service, Google may use what you type to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. Please don't type personal details into Ask Item cart AI. Your conversation with it is kept only on your own device for a few hours and then deleted. It is not saved on our servers.

6. Messages between users

Messages are stored so both people can read them. Messages that appear to share contact details are flagged, and our team may read flagged messages to keep the platform safe and to investigate complaints.

7. Cookies and storage on your device

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart, remember the phone number for an order you just placed (until you close the tab), and keep your Ask Item cart AI conversation for a few hours. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. How long we keep it

We keep order, payment and refund records for as long as needed to run the service, resolve disputes and meet legal and accounting requirements. Account details are kept while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account; we may keep records we are required to keep.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we no longer need it, object to certain uses, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To use any of these rights, email . We may need to confirm who you are first.

10. Security

Access to order and payment records is limited to authorised staff and protected by sign-in and access rules in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

11. Children

Item cart is meant for university students and other adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before using it, and don't use Ask Item cart AI.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you on the site.
